How to play

The board
Four colors share a twenty-by-twenty grid. Each color holds twenty-one tiles made of one to five squares.
First tile
Ember, Tide, Iris, and Flint each own one corner. Your first tile must cover your own corner.
Later tiles
Every new tile of yours must touch your existing tiles at a corner and must not share a side with your own color. Other colors may sit against your sides.
Scoring
When nobody can place, leftover squares on unplayed tiles cost one point each. Empty your entire set for fifteen points. Empty the set and finish with the single square for five more. Highest total wins.

Common questions

Does the game use data or show ads?
No. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no account, and no data collection. See the privacy policy.
Can I play with other people on this phone?
Yes. Switch to Hotseat and pass the phone. Field mode plays Ember against three on-device opponents.
