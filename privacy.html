Privacy Policy

AngleBlock · Effective October 1, 2026

AngleBlock collects no personal data. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising identifier, and no third-party tracking code in the app.

What the app stores

On your device only: your settings (sound, haptics, theme and similar), your stats and achievements, your Daily Quarry streak, and a match in progress so you can resume it. This is kept in the app's local storage and is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. Resetting stats in Settings, or deleting the app, removes it.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no server that receives data from the app, and we have no way to identify you or your device. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, and no analytics SDKs. The game makes no network requests, on iPhone, on Android, or in the browser version.

Permissions

On Android the app uses the vibration permission for haptic feedback. It requests no camera, location, contacts, microphone or other permissions on any platform.

Children

AngleBlock is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Any change that involves collecting data would ship with an app update and updated store privacy disclosures.
